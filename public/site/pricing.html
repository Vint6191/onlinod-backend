QUESTIONS

A direct price.
Direct answers.

Can I select Starter for any creator?

No. The standard tier follows that creator’s verified revenue. $1,000 enters Growth, $5,000 enters Pro and $15,000 enters Elite. The calculator is only an estimate; actual pricing is server-side.

Is the trial for each new creator?

The source issues a trial when a new agency is created. Adding a creator or accepting an invitation does not create another agency trial. The 14 days shown are the source default, not a live policy read.

What happens when the trial ends?

Creator operations require an active paid entitlement after the trial. Access to billing is separate so the owner can restore access. Planned free-module terms still need to be finalised.

Does topping up activate every creator?

No. The agency balance is shared, but creator subscriptions are separate. A confirmed balance top-up and a subscription purchase are different steps.

What if I disable auto-renewal?

The already paid period stays active until its end. Disabling renewal does not cancel that period immediately.

Are these final published prices?

No. This page is a design preview populated from the supplied Backend’s initial commercial configuration. Live prices and trial duration can be changed globally. No real checkout is connected here.
