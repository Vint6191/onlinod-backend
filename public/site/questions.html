QUESTIONS / 01

Popular questions.

What is NOD, and who is it for?

NOD is a desktop workspace for agencies managing OnlyFans creators. Conversations, fan CRM, Vault, campaigns, automation and team reporting sit alongside the work.

The website introduces the tools and access options. The day-to-day creator workflow belongs in the desktop app — not in this website preview.

Explore NOD
Where does an agency owner start?

Create your NOD account and agency, verify your email, then install the desktop app and sign in with the same account.

Connect the creator’s OnlyFans account in the desktop. Invite your team and assign the creators and permissions they need. Creating the agency does not automatically connect a creator.

Create an agency
Does a team member need to create another agency?

No. Join the agency through its invitation. Use your own NOD account, accept the invitation and work with the creators assigned to you.

A missing creator is a reason to check your assignment with the owner, not to create a duplicate agency. Joining a team does not issue another agency trial.

Join with an invitation
Why can a paid creator still need to reconnect?

OnlyFans connection and NOD access are separate states. A subscription enables NOD tools for that creator; it does not sign in to the creator’s OnlyFans account.

If the app asks for a connection or reconnection, complete that flow for the correct creator in the desktop. Paying for another period does not repair a lost session.

Do I need to verify my email? What if I forget my password?

Registration includes email verification before sign-in. The NOD email and password belong to your NOD account, not to a creator’s OnlyFans account.

Use password recovery from the login page if you lose access. In this preview the forms demonstrate the steps; verification and recovery emails are not sent.

Password recovery
Is the price calculated per agency or per creator?

Core access is priced for each creator separately. The standard revenue tier uses that creator’s verified revenue from the previous 30 completed UTC days, not the agency’s combined revenue.

The shared agency balance pays for separate creator subscriptions. Optional modules are also selected per creator. Pricing includes a calculator to explore the model.

Calculate your models
Can I choose Starter for a higher-earning creator?

No. The standard tier is calculated automatically: Starter, Growth, Pro or Elite. These are revenue tiers, not a menu where every creator can select the cheapest option.

If verified revenue is missing, pricing needs the data. Missing revenue must not be treated as zero. Prices shown on this preview use the supplied source configuration, not a live quote.

View pricing rules
How does the trial work?

The trial starts when a new agency is created. Adding another creator or accepting an invitation does not start a separate agency trial.

The supplied starting configuration sets it to 14 days. The live duration is controlled by the commercial policy and must be confirmed before launch. Check Pricing for the conditions shown in this preview.

Trial and pricing
What happens after the trial ends?

Creator operations require an active paid entitlement once the agency trial expires. Access is checked for the particular creator; paying for one does not enable every creator in the agency.

The owner can still use the billing path to restore access. The planned free modules have separate launch conditions that have not yet been finalised.

Does a balance top-up activate all creators?

No. A top-up and a subscription purchase are different steps. A confirmed payment credits the agency wallet. An active period must then exist for each creator you want to use.

A pending invoice is not a confirmed wallet credit. This design preview does not accept payments or activate subscriptions.

Will turning off renewal end the paid period immediately?

No. Turning off auto-renewal does not end the already paid period early. Access remains until the period’s stated end.

Renewal and the current paid entitlement are separate states. Check the creator’s period end, not only the auto-renewal switch.

Can I run the whole workspace in this website?

The creator workspace is a desktop app. The website covers the product, access, pricing and setup. The interactive examples here are not connected accounts.

Install NOD and sign in to work with your agency. Installing the app alone does not connect a creator or activate a subscription.

Open Download
Why doesn’t the download button download an installer yet?

This is a site preview. A verified release link has not been supplied, so the button explains the release status rather than starting a fake download.

Version, file size, supported system and the verified download link belong to the published build. They are deliberately not invented here.

See download status
Which systems and hardware are supported?

The Download design includes Windows and macOS choices, but that is not confirmation of released builds for both systems.

Supported OS versions, processor architecture, memory and disk requirements must be published with tested releases. The development package version is not a public release number.

Build information
Will desktop workflows run while my computer is off?

A desktop workflow needs an available runtime. A background process and an open application window are not the same thing, but switching off the computer stops local execution.

Sign-in, connected accounts and server functions also need internet access. Do not interpret “desktop” as fully offline, or a closed window as a guarantee that every job is still running.

Does “Unsorted” mean that a file has never been sent?

No. Sorting and usage history answer different questions. Unsorted helps find media outside the folder structure. Usage history helps see what was sent or purchased.

A file can already have been used and still need organising. Equally, an incomplete collected history is not proof that a file has never been sent.

Explore Vault
What can I see and manage inside Vault?

Organise folders, upload and select media for batch actions, and inspect sales-related information beside the content: sale count, attributed revenue, buyers and last sale.

Attributed revenue is not always a standalone sale price. Media can be sold in a bundle. Read the metric with its calculation method and available history instead of treating every file as an independent sale.

Can I send an offer based on content preferences?

Build a recipient list from CRM context: content interests, expressed requests, language and location, with exclusions for explicit refusals.

Two fans with the same spend can want completely different things. Review the recipient list before sending. The app rechecks the audience before creating the mass-message queue; a matching word alone is not enough to establish interest.

Explore content-aware campaigns
What if a fan said no, or their preference is unknown?

“I don’t like this” is not a positive interest. Use the CRM context and exclusions to distinguish an expressed request, an explicit refusal and missing information.

Do not treat an unknown preference as consent or a likely match. Review recipients; fewer relevant offers can be a better choice than sending every format to everyone.

Why can’t an employee see a particular creator?

Creator visibility depends on the employee’s assigned accounts and permissions. Ask the owner or an authorised manager to check the assignment and membership state.

NOD access, creator connection and team permissions are separate checks. Paying for the creator does not automatically assign that creator to every employee.

What does team reporting show beyond revenue?

Response-time measures, including median and P90, sit alongside unanswered dialogs. The reporting also covers confirmed posts and stories, manual activity and automation.

A fast average does not prove that everyone received a reply. Compare shifts with account load and traffic in mind; missing or updating data should not be read as zero work.

Explore team analytics
What does SFS Hunter automate?

It supports a promotion workflow: finding candidates from feed promotions, inspecting profiles, posts and comments, then running configured actions with limits and a history.

This is a way to reduce repetitive manual checks, not a guarantee of traffic or sales. Counts of follows, likes or comments are not the same as acquired customers.

Optional modules
Can I control the timing and pauses of automated messages?

Bump and follow workflows use configured templates, intervals, reply cooldowns and queue/history views. Check the scenario before enabling it; “automatic” does not mean sending without limits.

Connected accounts, permissions and an available desktop runtime are still required for desktop execution. Local demo switches on this website do not start real workflows.

The creator does not offer calls. Will NOD flag the request?Planned

That is the intended purpose of Creator Boundaries. The creator’s own services and limits will be visible in the conversation, with a warning when an incoming request may contradict them.

This is not just a platform banned-word list. The same call request can be outside one creator’s offer and compatible with another’s conditions. The aim is to warn before an unfulfillable promise is made.

The feature is planned. The profiles and warnings shown on this site are scripted examples, not a released recognition system or an automatic sales block.

See the boundaries concept
Does the translator work immediately after installation?

Incoming and outgoing translation uses a configured, available AI model. In the supplied desktop implementation the provider is Ollama; enable the translator and select the intended language and model.

Installing NOD is not the same as installing and configuring the AI model. If translation is unavailable, check those settings and model availability rather than sending the message unchecked.

Are AI suggestions the same as an autonomous chatter?

No. CRM summaries and suggested replies assist a person and depend on the configured model and available history. They are not a promise that an AI will run every conversation independently.

AI Chatter is a separate planned module in this preview. Review suggestions before using them; do not treat a generated assumption as a confirmed fan preference or creator commitment.

How does NOD help keep custom orders from being forgotten?

Record the order with its conversation, creator, brief, references, deadline and payment record. Pass the task through the configured Telegram connection and keep reminders and delivery status in view.

First, the order must be created. NOD is not presented as automatically recognising every promise in every chat. The payment record is not payment processing, and a reminder is not proof that the creator completed the work.

See the custom order workflow
Which tools are planned to be free?Planned

The launch plan is to make CRM, the translator and custom orders free. Exact access conditions, limits and AI-resource requirements still need to be confirmed.

That does not mean the entire NOD workspace is free after the trial, or that an AI model is included without setup. This preview has no active checkout or free-plan subscription.
