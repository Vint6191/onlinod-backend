QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

What is NOD?

NOD is a desktop workspace for OnlyFans agencies. It brings together fan CRM, Vault, message scripts, segmented mass messages, automation, custom orders and team analytics.

Which modules will be free?

We plan to make CRM, translation and custom orders free. Access conditions and AI-resource requirements will be published with pricing. This does not mean all of NOD is free.

Is this a connected account?

No. The people, conversations and figures on this page are fictional examples. Demo messages stay in this browser tab.

Which platform is NOD built for?

This version is built around OnlyFans accounts. NOD is a desktop workspace for agencies, not another content subscription platform.

Does the AI chat on its own?

The implemented AI tools assist with CRM analysis, reply suggestions and translation through a configured model. We are not presenting autonomous AI Chatter as a ready feature.

Are the analytics always complete?

Reports depend on collected history and synchronized data. The product tracks freshness and coverage; incomplete data should not be read as a guaranteed zero.

What is Telegram used for?

For creator task delivery, references and configurable custom-order reminders after the workspace connection is configured. NOD also records custom-order deadlines and payments.
